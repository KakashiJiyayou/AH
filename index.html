Asif Hasan

Artificial Intelligence · Computer Vision · Robotics

Hello, I’m Asif.

I am a Research Assistant at the Shenzhen Research Institute, Hong Kong Metropolitan University, working on AI for robotic Chinese painting.

My interests bring together artificial intelligence, computer vision, and robotics. At Ningbo University, I worked on helping unmanned boats detect obstacles and understand their surroundings using cameras. I have also worked on autonomous drones and built the Octopus IoT cloud platform to control smart devices.

I am now exploring vision-language models, world models, and reinforcement learning for robotics. In the long run, I hope to apply AI and robotics to sustainable agriculture, food security, and protecting the environment, alongside people who care about improving lives and our planet.

Outside research, I enjoy cycling and exploring the city, reading, keeping fish, cooking occasionally, and making smart toys.
